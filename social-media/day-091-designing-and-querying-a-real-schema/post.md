Day 91 of 365 Days of AI — Designing and Querying a Real Schema

Take a paragraph of prose from somebody who has never heard the word "schema" and turn it into a running database that answers real questions — mapping each phrase to an entity, an attribute, a relationship or something derived.

What today covers:
→ Map a paragraph of requirements to entities
→ Choose a surrogate primary key while keeping the natural key as a UNIQUE constraint
→ Recognise when a many-to-many junction table needs an attribute of its own

The lab is the point: 35 minutes of hands-on work you run yourself, offline, with output you can check against a real captured run.
