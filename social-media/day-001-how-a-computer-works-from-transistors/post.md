Day 1 of 365 Days of AI — How a Computer Works: From Transistors to Programs

Explain, layer by layer, how billions of on/off switches become running programs — and use that mental model to reason about AI hardware, memory limits, and cost.

What today covers:
→ Explain how transistors combine into logic gates
→ Describe the von Neumann architecture and why the stored-program idea makes one machine general-purpose
→ Trace the fetch-decode-execute cycle step by step for a concrete ADD instruction

The lab is the point: 30 minutes of hands-on work you run yourself, offline, with output you can check against a real captured run.
