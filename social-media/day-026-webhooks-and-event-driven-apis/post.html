Day 26 of 365 Days of AI — Webhooks and Event-Driven APIs

After this lesson you will understand the shift from pull to push: what a webhook is, how to receive one safely by verifying a signature, how retries and idempotency keep delivery reliable, and how these ideas open onto event-driven architecture.

What today covers:
→ Explain the difference between polling and webhooks
→ Describe a webhook as a "reverse API"
→ Verify a webhook by recomputing an HMAC signature over the raw body with a shared secret

The lab is the point: 30 minutes of hands-on work you run yourself, offline, with output you can check against a real captured run.
