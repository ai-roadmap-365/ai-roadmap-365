Day 61 of 365 Days of AI — Writing Readable Code

Write and refactor Python that other people (and your future self) can read: PEP 8 naming and layout, meaningful names and small single-purpose functions, docstrings that say why (PEP 257), type hints as checkable documentation, the Zen of Python (PEP 20) as guiding principles, and the tools that automate style — a formatter (Black) and a linter (Ruff or flake8).

What today covers:
→ Apply PEP 8 essentials without pedantry: naming conventions
→ Choose meaningful
→ Use the Zen of Python (PEP 20) as practical guidance for choosing between competing designs

The lab is the point: 30 minutes of hands-on work you run yourself, offline, with output you can check against a real captured run.
