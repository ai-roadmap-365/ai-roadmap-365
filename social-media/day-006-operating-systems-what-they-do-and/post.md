Day 6 of 365 Days of AI — Operating Systems: What They Do and Why

Explain what an operating system does — kernel vs user space, system calls, processes, virtual memory, filesystems, drivers, and permissions — and use that model to explain why AI and server work runs on Linux and to interrogate your own OS from the terminal.

What today covers:
→ Explain why bare hardware is unusable without an operating system
→ Distinguish kernel space from user space and describe how the CPU's privilege modes enforce the boundary in hardware
→ Trace a system call end to end

The lab is the point: 30 minutes of hands-on work you run yourself, offline, with output you can check against a real captured run.
