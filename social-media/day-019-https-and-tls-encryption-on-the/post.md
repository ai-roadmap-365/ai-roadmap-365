Day 19 of 365 Days of AI — HTTPS and TLS: Encryption on the Wire

Explain how HTTPS secures a web connection — the handshake, the mix of symmetric and public-key encryption, and the certificate chain of trust — and use openssl and curl to inspect any site's certificate and diagnose common TLS errors.

What today covers:
→ Explain the three protections TLS provides
→ Distinguish symmetric from public-key (asymmetric) encryption and explain why TLS combines them: slow public-key setup
→ Trace the TLS handshake step by step
