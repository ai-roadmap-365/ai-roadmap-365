Day 38 of 365 Days of AI — Regular Expressions

Read and write regular expressions with confidence — describing the shape of text with literals, character classes, anchors, quantifiers, groups, and alternation — and use them from the command line to search, extract, and reformat real data.

What today covers:
→ Explain what a regular expression is: a compact language that describes a set of strings by their shape
→ Read and write the core syntax
→ Use capture groups and backreferences to extract fields and reformat text

The lab is the point: 30 minutes of hands-on work you run yourself, offline, with output you can check against a real captured run.
