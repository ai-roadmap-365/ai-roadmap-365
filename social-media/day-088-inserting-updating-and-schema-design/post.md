Day 88 of 365 Days of AI — Inserting, Updating, and Schema Design

Change data without destroying it: writing rows in every useful form — single, multi-row, INSERT INTO SELECT, RETURNING and UPSERT via ON CONFLICT — updating with expressions and subqueries rather than stale literals, choosing between a hard DELETE and a reversible soft delete, and wrapping any of it in a transaction whose rollback you can prove leaves the database byte for byte as it was.

What today covers:
→ Measure the real cost of a WHERE-less UPDATE
→ Write every useful shape of INSERT
→ Update with an expression or a correlated subquery rather than a literal read earlier

The lab is the point: 30 minutes of hands-on work you run yourself, offline, with output you can check against a real captured run.
